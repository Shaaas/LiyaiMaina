Who We Are

A practice built against the grain.

Q-Rator is a Nairobi-based multidisciplinary design practice founded by Liyai Maina. The work spans brand identity, editorial design, pattern systems, and cultural advocacy.

The name is deliberate. A curator selects what enters. Q-Rator questions who is doing the selecting — and builds tools to shift that balance.

Every project is approached as both a design problem and a cultural one. Who is this for? Who does it include? What does it make possible?

The Approach

Strategy first. Aesthetics second.

The practice doesn't separate thinking from making. Strategy and visual form develop together — because the best brand systems are arguments, not just looks.

Work is grounded in research, built around clarity, and finished to a level that demands to be taken seriously.
